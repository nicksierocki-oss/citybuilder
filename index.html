How to play

  1. Extend the highway (grey road at the west edge) with the Road tool. Only roads connected to it count.
  2. Zone beside roads: Residential, Commercial, Industrial. Drag to paint rectangles.
  3. Watch the RCI meter: homes need jobs, shops need residents, industry needs workers & customers.
  4. Raise land value with parks and waterfront; keep industry away from homes to reach high density.
  5. Keep traffic moving: workers drive to the nearest open jobs. Long or jammed commutes make homes decline. Upgrade busy streets to Avenues (7), which carry 3× the traffic.
  6. Mind the budget: roads cost upkeep. Six months in debt and you're fired.
