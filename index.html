How to play

  1. Extend the regional road (the avenue entering from the west edge). Only roads connected to a map edge count.
  2. Zone beside roads: Residential, Commercial, Industrial. Drag to paint rectangles.
  3. Watch demand (bottom-left): homes need jobs, shops need residents, industry needs workers & customers.
  4. Power & water: build a wind farm or coal plant and a water pump (best near the river) beside a road. They flow along roads. Buildings need power for medium density and water for high.
  5. Keep people happy with schools, clinics, plazas, parks and clean air. Recycling centers and trees cut pollution.
  6. Stay safe: police stations cut crime (dense, run-down and jobless areas attract it). Fire stations prevent fires and put them out; without one nearby a fire can spread and burn buildings down. Click a fire alert to jump to it.
  7. Keep traffic moving: drag the Upgrade tool (9) along busy roads: Street → Avenue → Highway (no driveways).
  8. Mind the budget: roads and public buildings cost upkeep. Six months in debt and you're fired.
